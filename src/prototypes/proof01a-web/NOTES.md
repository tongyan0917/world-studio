Concrete mixed direction, if any:

> Use a World Evolution Workbench as the default shell: a semantically zoomable macro–meso–micro history is the primary object; selecting a transition synchronizes World state, people and Organizations, causal trace, and Why-Not evidence. Keep Wiki, Markdown, and object pages as secondary inspection and creator-input tools. Absorb the causal explanation strengths of B and compact comparison strengths of C without retaining three competing product surfaces.

Timeline content combines continuous Processes and state trends, consequential Events and actions, and a concise trace-grounded stage summary at every visible scale. Summary sentences cite validated Explanation Claims and remain a read-only presentation layer; they cannot create World Facts, causal parents, or hidden completion of unresolved history.

The default summary is an Audit Explanation. A perspective selector may instead project one Actor's contemporaneous Perspective Explanation, an Organizational Explanation that separates declaration, decision, and implementation, or a Social Memory View of later circulating and recorded accounts. These views remain visibly distinct and may contradict one another; the interface never blends them into a single omniscient narration.

Selecting a timeline node exposes four explicit operations: inspect committed history, request Resolution Promotion around a subject and its causal context, create an Anchored Run from that point, or apply a provenance-visible Author Intervention. There is no generic edit action that silently overwrites committed history; presentation annotations may be edited only because they carry no World-state authority.

Creating an Anchored Run preserves both its base Possible History and the new branch and opens them as a comparison; recency never makes the new branch authoritative. A creator may explicitly and reversibly designate one branch as the Working Continuity for subsequent work without invalidating, rewriting, or deleting the others.

Branch comparison defaults to a Run Difference Explanation: shared causal prefix, changed input or version, first material divergence, recorded downstream propagation, unchanged protected state, and blocked paths. A complete field-level diff remains available as a secondary audit tool but is not the primary way to understand why histories differ.

Every person, Organization, Place, and other object inspector is bound to the selected Inspection Time. It presents only the state, relationships, resources, plans, and accessible information valid then, plus a trace of how that state formed; later facts remain hidden until the creator moves Inspection Time, and conditional future views begin only from a History Head rather than leaking hindsight into the snapshot.

Relationship graph edges are discovery and navigation aids, not complete relationship state. Selecting one opens its time-scoped Relational Roles, shared auditable Interaction History, separate A-to-B and B-to-A Relationship Models, and the accessible information, Recollections, and appraisals behind each change; an edge never turns role, interaction, or co-membership into symmetric affinity by itself.

An Organization inspector follows a declaration–internal decision–implementation–consequence chain. It keeps declared purpose, public rationale, member roles and interests, resources and information, agenda and coalition dynamics, formal decision, transmitted instructions, compliance or resistance, observed behavior, consequences, and outside interpretations separate; the interface never summarizes these as one hidden organizational motive.

Institution and Organization are separate linked inspectors. An Institution view follows norms and customs, interpretations, recognized Claims and obligations, stated rules versus practice, legitimacy, compliance, enforcement capacity, jurisdiction, and historical change, while Organization views identify the changing people and structures that currently interpret, maintain, exploit, resist, or replace it; either may persist or transform without the other.

The first MVP uses a causal spatial view rather than a full cartographic editor. It renders Places, Regions, Routes, travel time, capacity, access, control and Jurisdiction, trade and migration flows, closures, and Hazard footprints from the selected World State; geometry may remain schematic, but reachability and propagation cannot be decorative or presentation-authored.

The first creator setup keeps one bounded route-dependent settlement, grain-reserve Institution, and extreme-winter scenario skeleton. It exposes only structured inputs for initial Population and resources, Route and environmental trends, reserve rules, external Boundary Conditions, Hazard seed, Simulation Focus, and optional Creative Guidance; every accepted change creates a Contract Version and comparable Run. Arbitrary-world authoring, free-form Markdown compilation, general rule editing, and full fantasy construction remain outside this MVP.

## Verdict

- [x] Direction is clear enough to carry into the next MVP pass.
- [ ] Run one small revision against a named confusion.
- [ ] Reject these structures and reframe the visual question.

Chosen direction:

World Evolution Workbench as the first MVP's default entry, with one bounded history from settlement formation through institutional evolution, the extreme-winter micro window, and its long aftermath.

Reason:

Opening on an evolving history makes the World Engine's primary product claim visible; opening on Wiki pages or an isolated character editor would make authored records appear primary and hide cross-scale causal evolution.
